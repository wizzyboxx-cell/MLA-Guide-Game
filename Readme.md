# Citation Island

An island adventure that teaches students MLA 9 citation for music sources. Students explore eight zones, from Soundcheck Shore to Volcano Summit. Each zone teaches one skill with a short lesson and quick challenges, and clearing a zone lifts the fog from the next one.

Made for music students at ISHCMC by Mr Wisdom.

All the patterns in one place: [MLA Citation Snapshot for Musicians](https://claude.ai/artifact/YV8b9CZNQaKhi6Ys6xSXJ4)

## Levels

Students choose their level first. Each level has its own route and saves its own progress.

| Level | Grades | Zones |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explorer | Grades 6 to 8 (MYP 1 to 3) | 7 |
| Navigator | Grades 9 to 10 (MYP 4 to 5) | 9 |
| Captain | Grades 11 to 12 (IB Diploma) | 10 |

## Zones

1. Soundcheck Shore: why we cite and how the Works Cited page looks
2. Bookwreck Cove: books with one, two or more authors
3. Signal Tower: citing a website, step by step
4. Lighthouse Point: encyclopedias and databases (Navigator and Captain)
5. Echo Caves: songs, albums and liner notes
6. Waterfall Stage: YouTube videos and live concerts
7. Score Temple: musical scores (Navigator and Captain)
8. Quotation Jungle: in-text citations, quotes, paraphrases and timestamps
9. Research Ridge: IB academic integrity, AI tools and figures (Captain only)
10. Volcano Summit: the final mixed challenge

## Running it

The whole game is one file, `index.html`. Open it in any browser, or publish it with GitHub Pages:

1. Upload `index.html` and this README to a GitHub repository.
2. Go to **Settings > Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, pick `main` and the `/ (root)` folder, then select **Save**.
4. After a minute the game is live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repository-name>/`.

Progress is saved in each student's own browser.
